Article 23 — Reporting obligations

Entities shall notify the CSIRT or competent authority of any incident having
a significant impact on the provision of their services.

(1) Early warning — without undue delay and in any event within 24 hours of
    becoming aware of the significant incident.
(4) Final report — not later than one month after the incident notification,
    including a detailed description, severity, impact, and mitigation applied.
